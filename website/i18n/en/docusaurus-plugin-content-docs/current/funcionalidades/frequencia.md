# Attendance (roll call)

:::tip Delivered in M2
Roll call per class, excused absences and attendance per subject and overall on the report card already work.
:::

Under the Brazilian education law (LDB), a student needs **75% attendance** in the year to pass (the school can set another value in the
average rule). In Caderneta, roll call takes seconds.

## How it works

- The teacher opens **Home → Roll call** and taps **Record roll call**. The date is already filled in with today.
- **Everyone starts present.** Just tap **F** (absent), **J** (excused absence) or **A** (late) for whoever
  needs it.
- Double classes count as two (the **Classes** field); the lesson content is optional.
- Roll call cannot be recorded for a future date or outside the school year; a closed term can only be corrected by the principal's office.
- Only absences and lateness are stored: marking someone back as present deletes the record.

## Calculation

- Subject attendance = (classes given − absences) ÷ classes given.
- **An excused absence still counts as an absence** for attendance: the LDB only excuses special cases (such as the special
  study regime). It appears separately on the report card so the school can follow it.
- Lateness does not count as an absence.
- Each student's attendance appears in the gradebook and on the report card; overall attendance adds up all subjects.

- [x] Roll call per class in a few taps for a whole class present
- [x] Attendance percentage per subject and overall on the report card
- [x] Excused absence with a recorded reason
- [x] Automatic alerts to the family and coordination at 15% and 20% absences (M3, [Communication](./comunicacao.md))
- [x] Days off and holidays on the calendar block roll call (M4, [Calendar](./calendario.md))
