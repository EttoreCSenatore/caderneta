# School, classes and staff

:::tip Delivered in M1
Everything on this page already works.
:::

## School dashboard

The principal's office and coordination start from the dashboard: number of active students, classes, teachers and pending invitations, and
the school year's terms with the current one highlighted.

![Principal's dashboard](/img/screenshots/admin-inicio.png)

## School year and subjects

Under **School**, the principal's office creates the school year choosing two-month terms, three-month terms or semesters. Caderneta splits the dates
automatically; the school's subjects are on the same screen.

![School year and subjects](/img/screenshots/escola.png)

## Classes and assignments

Each class belongs to a school year. Under **Teachers**, the principal's office links a teacher to a subject of the class: this
**assignment** defines which students the teacher can see.

![Class list](/img/screenshots/turmas.png)

![Teachers of a class](/img/screenshots/turma-professores.png)

## What the teacher sees

Teachers only see the classes where they have an assignment and, inside them, the **Health alerts** tab with the essential summary
defined by the school (without the full medical report).

![Teacher home](/img/screenshots/professor-inicio.png)

![Class health alerts](/img/screenshots/professor-alertas.png)

## Staff

A list of principal's office, coordination and teachers, showing who has already enabled 2FA, and the invitations not yet accepted (which the principal's office
can cancel). Deactivating someone removes their access immediately, without erasing the history.

![School staff](/img/screenshots/equipe.png)
