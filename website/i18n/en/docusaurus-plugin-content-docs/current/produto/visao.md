# Product vision

## The problem

In many schools, mainly small and mid-sized ones, school life is scattered:

- grades in paper gradebooks or spreadsheets that only the teacher sees, and averages computed by hand at the end of the term;
- parents who only find out about a low grade when the report card is handed out, when it is too late to help;
- notices lost in crowded WhatsApp groups, with no way to know who read them;
- exams sent by e-mail or USB stick to the office to be printed, with no control over deadlines or quantities;
- health information and emergency contacts in a folder at the office, far from whoever is with the student;
- every year, the student's history and observations are lost when the teacher changes.

Existing school management systems tend to be expensive, heavy and designed for the office, not for the teacher
and the family.

## The proposal

A platform that is **simple, cheap to operate and pleasant on the phone**, focused on the daily life of four groups:
school, teachers, guardians and students.

## Goals

| Goal | How we measure it |
|---|---|
| Teachers enter grades faster than in a spreadsheet | Enter 30 grades in under 2 minutes |
| Families follow closely | 70% of guardians log in at least once a month in the pilot |
| Notices really arrive | 80% read confirmation on important notices within 48 h |
| Print queue without friction | Zero exams delivered late by the office in the pilot |
| Works on any phone | First screen loaded in under 2 s on 3G; initial bundle under 200 KB |

## Out of scope (for now)

- Finance (tuition, invoices, delinquency).
- Distance learning, video lessons and assignment submission with online grading (ideas for after the pilot).
- Integration with official secretariat systems (Educacenso, SED etc.): evaluated after the pilot.
- Real-time chat between parents. Messages are always school/teacher ↔ family.

## Ideas added to the original scope

Beyond the initial request, the plan includes items that a Brazilian school misses in daily life:

- **Attendance (roll call)** per class, with an alert when the student gets close to the 25% absence limit.
- **Read confirmation** ("acknowledged") from guardians on notices and messages.
- **Digital authorizations** (field trip, early dismissal) that the guardian approves in the app.
- **Configurable average rule** per school: arithmetic or weighted average, recovery, rounding.
- **Report card as PDF** to download or print.
- **Spreadsheet (CSV) import** of students, guardians and classes, so nobody types everything from scratch.
- **Bulk re-enrollment**: promote a whole class to the next year carrying the history.
- **Audit log** of who viewed or changed grades and sensitive data.
